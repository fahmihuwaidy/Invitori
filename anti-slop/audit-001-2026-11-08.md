# Anti-Slop Audit Report #001
**Date:** 2026-11-08  
**Scope:** Whole Web App (Landing Page, Auth, Dashboard, Editor, Public Invitation, Contact)  
**Evaluator:** Antislop Audit Mode (AFTER)

---

## Findings List

### Group 1: Hard Gate (Priority: HIGH)

1. **R-03 (Mobile Responsiveness) — Broken Mobile Menu in Landing Page**
   - **Location:** `src/pages/LandingPage.jsx:L60-65`
   - **Reason:** The mobile hamburger button toggles state, but the container has `style={{ display: 'none' }}` with no links inside, leaving mobile navigation completely broken.

2. **R-02 (Copywriting) — Forbidden Em Dash in Marketing Copy**
   - **Location:** `src/pages/LandingPage.jsx:L162`
   - **Reason:** Uses em dash `— Juliana Thorne, Lead Creator` in creator quote; em dashes are strictly forbidden in agent-written copy.

3. **R-18 & R-36 (Testimonials & Fabricated Claims) — Fictional Customer Review**
   - **Location:** `src/pages/LandingPage.jsx:L177-186`
   - **Reason:** Displays fictional testimonial attributed to `"Eleanor Vance, Florence Summer Society"` without verified real customer proof.

4. **R-24 & R-26 (Navigation & Interactive Elements) — Dead Footer Links**
   - **Location:** `src/pages/LandingPage.jsx:L210-216`
   - **Reason:** Links for "Privacy Policy", "Terms of Service", "Instagram", and "Pinterest" point to dead `#` anchors.

5. **R-26 (Interactive Elements) — Dead Form and Social Login Buttons**
   - **Location:** `src/pages/LoginPage.jsx:L141-143, L172-174`
   - **Reason:** "FORGOT?" password button and "Apple" OAuth button have no `onClick` event handlers and do nothing when pressed.

6. **R-32 (Keyboard Accessibility) — Global Focus Outline Stripped Without Replacement**
   - **Location:** `src/index.css:L107`
   - **Reason:** `button { outline: none; }` eliminates focus rings globally without providing a custom `:focus-visible` indicator, breaking keyboard navigation.

7. **R-25 (Color Contrast) — Dusty Pink and Muted Grey Fail WCAG AA Standard**
   - **Location:** `src/pages/LandingPage.jsx:L135`, `src/pages/LandingPage.css:L17`
   - **Reason:** `--pink-dusty` (#d09ca7) on off-white background (#FAFAFA) yields a contrast ratio of ~2.2:1, failing the 4.5:1 minimum standard.

8. **R-36 (No Fabricated Claims) — False End-to-End Encryption Claim**
   - **Location:** `src/pages/LoginPage.jsx:L180`
   - **Reason:** Claims `🔒 End-to-end encryption enabled` while Supabase stores data as standard plaintext PostgreSQL records.

9. **R-38 (Real Content or Honest Placeholder) — Raw Lorem Ipsum in Production Contact Page**
   - **Location:** `src/pages/ContactPage.jsx:L32`
   - **Reason:** Contains Latin filler text `"Quisque velit nisi, pretium ut lacinia in, elementum id enim..."` in user-facing consultation description.

10. **R-27 (UI States) — Editor Page Crashes on Initial Direct Load (Missing Loading State)**
    - **Location:** `src/pages/EditorPage.jsx:L2453, L2472`
    - **Reason:** Directly reads `invitation.theme` and `invitation.title` without checking if `invitation` is loaded, crashing with `TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined` on fresh reload.

11. **R-26 (Interactive Elements) — Fullscreen Coming Soon Overlay Traps Visitors**
    - **Location:** `src/components/ComingSoon.jsx:L5, L48-109`
    - **Reason:** Blocks access to `LandingPage` unless the visitor happens to pass `?preview=true` or types `<>` on their keyboard.

12. **R-22 & R-38 (Visual Assets & Real Content) — CSS Placeholder Boxes in Place of Product Imagery**
    - **Location:** `src/pages/LandingPage.css:L170-176, L291`
    - **Reason:** Displays empty grey/stone boxes with CSS `content: "Hero Image Placeholder"` and `"Wedding Suite Image Placeholder"` despite available assets in `src/assets/`.

13. **R-26 (Interactive Elements) — Invalid HTML Button Inside Link Tag**
    - **Location:** `src/pages/LandingPage.jsx:L192-194`
    - **Reason:** `<Link to="/contact"><button className="btn-white">Begin Your Story</button></Link>` nests interactive elements, confusing screen readers and keyboard navigation.

### Group 2: Purpose-Gate (Priority: MEDIUM)

14. **R-04 & R-26 (Icons & Interactive Elements) — Non-Functional Symbol Glyphs With Pointer Cursors**
    - **Location:** `src/pages/LandingPage.jsx:L224-226`
    - **Reason:** `❖`, `❀`, `✑` symbols styled with `cursor: pointer` but have no actions or meanings.

15. **R-06 (Typography) — Illegible Micro-Typography in Caps**
    - **Location:** `src/pages/LandingPage.css:L17, L221`
    - **Reason:** Text set at 10px with `letter-spacing: 0.25em` becomes unreadable on mobile screens.

16. **R-08 (Button Arrows) — Generic Directional Arrow Without Clear Navigation Purpose**
    - **Location:** `src/pages/LandingPage.jsx:L81`
    - **Reason:** `The Collection &rarr;` uses default arrow glyph instead of contextual action label.
